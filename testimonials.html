Testimonials

In their words

Reflections from photographers and participants who have been part of the journey.

Working with Hossein requires a certain kind of honesty. Not because he asks for it, but because sooner or later you realize that photography can only take you as far as you are willing to go yourself. What begins as a conversation about images often turns into a conversation about the person making them.

Hossein has a rare ability to direct attention toward what is usually left unexplored. The questions beneath the photographs. The things we avoid, postpone, or struggle to name. In that sense, the work is not just about learning how to make better images. It is about seeing yourself more clearly.

An experience that continues to shape the way I work.

Teona MachavarianiCurator · Georgia
My session with Hossein was impactful right from the first day. He teaches in a way that no other photography course does: he first helps you find your inner artist.

Most photography classes focus on gear and technique, but very few guide you toward discovering your own creativity and unique talents. Once you get to know the artist within yourself, it no longer matters what gear or medium you use. Your work will always reflect your own voice, and that is what people will ultimately recognize and remember.

Talal MansoorPhotographer & Graphic Designer · India
After joining your sessions, I started looking at photography projects differently. Instead of seeing photos as individual images, I began thinking of them as part of a larger visual project, with a story, concept, and purpose behind it.

Before joining I expected something more technical. Looking back, I realize my perspective was limited. The discussions about careers, personal experiences, creative journeys, and real-world projects turned out to be just as valuable. Learning does not only come from camera settings; it also comes from hearing how others think, create, and solve problems.

Zeyad BashirPhotographer · Sudan
I really enjoyed Hossein's photography sessions because they were about discovering your own perspective and voice as a photographer. The atmosphere was always relaxed, friendly, and inspiring.

I appreciated how he encouraged us to think beyond taking "nice photos" and focus on creating meaningful stories and personal projects. One thing I especially enjoyed was seeing our photographs printed, experimenting with paper, texture, and how images can be presented. The sessions felt like a space for creativity, reflection, and growth.

Anna KorotinskaPhotographer · Ukraine
