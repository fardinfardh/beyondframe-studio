Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Beyond Frame collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website or book a session.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, WhatsApp number, country, portfolio links, and any notes you share when booking or contacting us. Payment is processed by Stripe; we do not store your card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to schedule and deliver your sessions, respond to enquiries, send your written proposal and follow-up summaries, and, if you opt in, send the free Direction Audit and occasional updates.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third parties to operate: Calendly (scheduling), Stripe (payment), Google Workspace (email), and MailerLite (email newsletter). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time.

5. Cookies & Analytics

The website may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email hello@beyondframe.studio.
