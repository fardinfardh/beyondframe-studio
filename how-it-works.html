How It Works

One Path. Built Around You.

There is no menu of programs here. Most mentorships ask you to choose a package before anyone has seen your work. Beyond Frame works the other way around: first we understand where you are, then you receive a written proposal for a program designed only for you.

Every mentorship begins with a Direction Session (USD 90). If you continue into a program, the 90 is deducted from your program price.

01

The Direction Session

A private 60-minute call on Google Meet. Before we meet, you send 10 to 15 images and a few notes about your goals and frustrations. In the session we look honestly at where you are, what is holding you back, and what genuinely deserves your focus next.

60 minutes · Online · USD 90 (credited toward your program)
02

Your Written Proposal

Within 48 hours of your session, you receive a one-page written proposal. It contains:

  • An honest summary of where you are now
  • The 2 to 3 areas your program will focus on
  • Number of sessions, cadence, and duration
  • The final deliverable you will leave with
  • The exact price, with the USD 90 already deducted

The proposal is valid for 14 days. There is no obligation, the Direction Session stands on its own.

03

Your Personal Program

Programs are built from the Four Pillars framework and shaped entirely around your needs. Every program includes:

  • Private 75-minute sessions on Google Meet
  • A written summary and assignment after every session
  • Ongoing feedback on your work between sessions
  • A concrete final deliverable: a direction statement, a refined portfolio, or a 12-month opportunity plan

Programs range from short and focused (4 to 6 sessions) to a full journey (up to 24 sessions over about 6 months). Most fall between USD 780 and USD 2,900. Payment in full or in installments via Stripe.

Before You Book

Questions, answered honestly.

How do sessions work?

Private, one to one, 75 minutes, live on Google Meet. Between sessions you get assignments, written feedback, and can reach me on WhatsApp or email.

Why is there no price list?

Because no two photographers need the same program. Your proposal states the exact price before you commit to anything.

What if I only want the Direction Session?

That is completely fine. It stands on its own, you leave with real clarity about your work and next step, with zero pressure to continue.

What is the refund policy?

Full details are on our Refund Policy page, summarised before you book, so there are no surprises.
