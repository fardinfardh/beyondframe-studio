Not sure what your next step is?

Get clarity on your work, your direction, and your next step in a private one-to-one session.

Sometimes the next step is not another course, portfolio review, or workshop. Sometimes you simply need clarity.

This session is for photographers who feel stuck, unsure what to focus on next, or uncertain about which path makes the most sense for them. Together, we look at where you are now, what is holding you back, and what your next step could be.

During the session, we look closely at your current situation, your work, your interests, and the questions you are facing. We talk through:

  • where you are in your photography today
  • what feels unclear, difficult, or frustrating
  • what you want to build, explore, or improve

By the end of the session, you will have a clearer understanding of:

  • your direction
  • your main gaps
  • what to focus on next
  • whether a focused program, a complete program, or independent work is right for you
  • greater confidence in your next step

This session stands on its own. Whether you continue with a program or not, you will leave with greater clarity about your work and your next step.

You don't need another course. You need clarity about what matters to you.
